### 📖 Introduction
**RefLog** (Referee Logger) was born out of my passion for football and my personal experience as a newly qualified referee. It is an independent practice project developed in Android Studio. My goal is to use modern design language to provide an intuitive and efficient tool for soccer referees.

### 📊 Dashboard
The home screen provides a core data view of your refereeing career, making it easy to quickly understand your overall performance.
*   **Multi-dimensional Statistics**: Summarizes basic metrics such as the ratio of red/yellow cards and average goals per match.
*   **Visualized Charts**: Introduces a Donut Chart to intuitively display the proportion of various penalty events.
*   **Recent Matches**: Quickly review the scores and records of recently completed matches.

### ⏱️ Unified Event Panel
Optimized traditional event recording interactions by introducing an aggregated bottom panel on the timer screen.
*   **Centralized Operation**: Within a single bottom sheet, you can sequentially complete the input of **"Event Type -> Team -> Player Number"**, avoiding the fragmentation caused by multi-level pop-ups.
*   **Non-blocking Design**: The panel height has been carefully adjusted so that the countdown timer at the top of the screen remains fully visible while recording events.

### 📁 Match Presets
Provides configuration templates for regular matches.
*   **Parameter Customization**: Supports pre-setting home and away team names, team colors, and half-time duration.
*   **Quick Start**: Simply tap the corresponding card in the preset list to load the configuration and instantly enter the timing state.

### 📝 Match History
Provides structured post-match data retention.
*   **Match Archives**: Automatically saves the final scores and basic configurations of completed matches.
*   **Timeline Details**: Accurately records the exact time and player number for every goal, red/yellow card, substitution, and injury, facilitating post-match verification and referee report generation.
